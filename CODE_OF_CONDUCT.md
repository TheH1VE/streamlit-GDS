# Code of conduct

We want participation in Streamlit GDS to be respectful, accessible, and
constructive. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with empathy and assume good intent;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability;
- give specific, actionable feedback about the work rather than the person;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or service-sensitive information;
- accept responsibility, apologise, and repair harm when mistakes happen; and
- prioritise user safety and accessibility in technical decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
